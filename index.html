Why Klyro is free

Klyro was built because we got tired of finding a simple tool online only to be asked to pay or create an account. So we made our own — no tricks, no paywalls, no annoying ads.

Every tool on Klyro runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your files are processed on your own device and never get uploaded to any server. That means your data stays private, and we have zero hosting costs for file processing — which is why we can keep everything free, forever.

Whether you need to compress an image, merge PDFs, generate a QR code, or convert units — just open the tool and go. No signup. No waiting. Just works.
